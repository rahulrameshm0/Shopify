Building India's most trusted tech store — one honest order at a time.

Started in 2023 from a garage in Bangalore. We're a small team obsessed with fixing what's broken about buying electronics online in India: fake warranties, swapped components, and support that disappears after the sale.

Why we exist

Last year, our founder Rahul ordered a "brand new" GPU from a major marketplace. It arrived with bent pins, a scratched backplate, and a warranty card that had already been registered to someone else in 2021. The seller ghosted. The platform said "contact the seller." The manufacturer said "not our problem — unauthorized reseller."

That's the story of almost every tech buyer in India. You either pay a premium at offline stores with limited stock, or you gamble on marketplaces where "new" is a suggestion, not a promise.

We started Shopify because that gamble shouldn't exist. We buy directly from brand-authorized distributors — Ingram, Redington, Rashi, Savex, Supertron. Every unit is inspected on camera before it leaves our Bangalore facility. Serial numbers are recorded and matched to your invoice. The warranty starts the day you receive it, not the day it left a warehouse in 2021.

We're bootstrapped, profitable from month three, and growing because customers come back. No VC pressure to chase GMV at the cost of trust.

What we do differently

📦

Open-box inspection on camera

Every high-value item (₹15,000+) is unboxed, powered on, stress-tested, and recorded on video. You get the video link with your tracking number. No swapped GPUs, no used SSDs sold as new.

🛡

Warranty that actually works

We're an authorized service partner for every brand we carry. If something fails, you email us — we arrange pickup, repair at the brand's service center, and delivery back. No "send it to the manufacturer yourself" runaround.

🤝

Direct distributor relationships

We buy from Ingram Micro, Redington, Rashi Peripherals, Savex, Supertron — the same distributors that supply Croma, Reliance Digital, and Vijay Sales. Valid serials, honored firmware updates, eligible for every manufacturer promo.

⚡

Same-day dispatch from Bangalore

Orders before 1 PM IST ship the same day via Blue Dart / Delhivery. Free shipping on orders above ₹2,999. COD available on select pin codes (we verify high-value COD orders with a quick call).

🔄

14-day no-questions returns

Changed your mind? Initiate from your account — we send a pickup. Refund to original payment method within 3–5 business days after we receive it at our facility. No restocking fees.

📞

Real humans, not chatbots

Support is WhatsApp, email, and phone — answered by our team in Bangalore who know the products because they test them. Average first reply: under 2 hours during business hours. No ticket numbers, no "we'll get back to you."

Our non-negotiables

1 No gray market, ever

If we can't get it from an authorized distributor, we don't sell it. Period. That means some popular models go out of stock — we'd rather lose the sale than sell you a unit with a void warranty.

2 Invoice = warranty proof

Every order gets a proper GST invoice with serial numbers listed. That invoice is your warranty proof with the brand — no separate registration, no "warranty card" that gets lost.

3 Right to repair respected

We stock genuine spares for the brands we carry (batteries, screens, fans, charging ports). Opening your laptop to add RAM or swap an SSD doesn't void your warranty with us.

4 No fake discounts

No "MRP ₹99,999 — our price ₹59,999" theater. We price at distributor cost + sustainable margin. If a brand drops prices, we drop ours the same day. No coupon codes, no flash sale pressure.
